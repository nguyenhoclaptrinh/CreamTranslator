# CreamTranslator: Customer User Guide & Quick Start

Thank you for choosing **CreamTranslator**. This guide explains how to install, set up, and get the most out of your lifetime screen translation tool.

---

## 1. Quick Installation (One-Time Setup)

1. **Install the APK:** Transfer `CreamTranslator-v1.0.0.apk` to your Android device and tap to install.
2. **Grant Overlay Permission:**
   - On the first app launch, tap **"Grant Overlay Permission"**.
   - In your Android Settings, toggle the switch to allow CreamTranslator to display over other apps.
3. **Download Language Pack:**
   - Tap **"Re-verify Offline Model"** to download the offline English-Vietnamese pack (~30MB).
   - Once completed, the status indicator will show **"Ready (Offline)"**.
4. **Start Service:**
   - Tap **"Start Translator Service"** and confirm the standard Android screen capture prompt.

---

## 2. Using CreamTranslator in Games

### Floating Trigger (Minimal Pill)
Once the service starts, a small circular pill docks cleanly to the edge of your screen. You can drag it to any convenient position.

### Modes of Operation
- **Single Tap (Full Screen):** Tap the pill once during any dialogue scene or menu. The app scans the screen, removes foreign text with matching background plates, and renders translated Vietnamese text directly in-place.
- **Tap Any Text to Toggle:** Tap on any translated sentence to toggle back to the original English text. Tap again to return to Vietnamese.
- **Region Translate:** Drag across the screen to draw a selection rectangle over specific dialog boxes or quest logs to translate only that designated area.
